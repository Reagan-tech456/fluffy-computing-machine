WELCOME TO GREENFIELD

Education that goes beyond the classroom.

Our sample school is imagined as part of a Kenyan community. We aim to provide a friendly learning environment where learners can ask questions, practise new skills and discover what they enjoy.

We value respect, responsibility, creativity and cooperation. We also believe that families and teachers work best when they work together.
